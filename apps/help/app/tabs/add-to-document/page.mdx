Sometimes a tab you built in one document belongs in another too: a glossary, a shared timeline, an architecture board two projects both rely on. **Add to Document** adds the active tab to another document you own, without leaving the editor.

## Adding a tab to another document

<Step n={1}>Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab).</Step>
<Step n={2}>Choose **Add to Document**.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  In the **Add to Document** dialog, pick the destination. Each document shows as a tile with a
  preview of its canvas, and the filter box at the top narrows a long list. A message confirms when
  the tab has been added.
</Step>

The option is greyed out when there is nowhere to add the tab to: if this is your only document, until you create another, in a [document in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), and on a [Plan](/help/canvas/plan-tabs/) tab, whose cards belong to this document's items. It isn't offered to Viewers or Participants, or on a link shared for [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/).

## One tab, shared between documents

Add to Document doesn't make an independent copy: the tab is shared, and the documents holding it keep it in step. Edit its elements, restyle it, or rename it from either document and the change reaches the other a few seconds after you stop editing. If the other document is already open in another window, reload it to see the latest edits. When two documents change the shared tab at almost the same moment, the later change wins in both.

Two things stay per-document, so each document can organise the shared tab its own way: the tab's position in the tab list, and which [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/) (if any) it sits in.

Removing the tab from one document doesn't touch the others: they keep the tab and its contents, and stop sharing edits with the document that removed it. Deleting a whole document works the same way: once it leaves the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) for good, every tab it shared stays in the other documents, and taking a document offline tells you how many tabs that is before you go ahead.

A document that already has a different tab under the same identity (for example one brought back from [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) after its tabs went their own way) can't take the tab; a message says it could not be added.

<Tip>
  Prefer an independent copy you can tailor per project? Use **Duplicate** in the same menu first,
  then add the duplicate to the other document.
</Tip>
