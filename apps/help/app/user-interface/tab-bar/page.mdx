A document can hold many boards, and each one is its own canvas in a **tab**. The tabs live in **the document group**, at the right of [the bottom line](/help/user-interface/title-bar/#the-bottom-line), straight after the document's name. Reading from left to right:

- **The active tab**: the tab you are on, with its mode's icon, its name and its **⋯** menu. Click the name to rename the tab.
- **The tab stack**: a stack icon and the number of tabs in the document. Click it to open **the tab list**.
- **The people here**: up to three avatars (initials or profile pictures) of the others in the document, then **+** and how many more. It opens the same tab list. When nobody else is here it is not shown.
- **+**: adds a tab.

## The tab list

The tab list is the one place every tab is seen and switched to. It opens above the button you clicked:

- At the top, **Tabs** and how many there are, and how many people are here when anyone else is.
- **Find a tab**, when the document has more than five tabs: type to narrow the list by name.
- Every tab, in the document's order, each with its icon, its name and a small stack of avatars showing who is on it right now. [Tab folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/) are headings, with their tabs indented under them. The active tab's row is tinted and carries its **⋯** menu.
- At the foot, **New Tab**.

Click a row to switch to that tab; the list closes. From the keyboard, the arrow keys move between the rows, `Enter` switches, and `Escape` closes the list and puts you back on the button that opened it.

## What you can do here

- **Switch** between boards by clicking a row in the tab list.
- **Add** a new tab with the **+** button's **Tab**, or **New Tab** in the tab list. It arrives numbered ("Tab 2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, in the same mode, on that mode's start, so you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the tab blank. Added from a Plan tab, it is a Structured tab. **Board**, **Sheet** and **Dashboard** in the same menu (**New Board**, **New Sheet** and **New Dashboard** in the tab list) add a [Plan tab](/help/canvas/plan-tabs/) on Start Planning.
- **Rename** a tab by clicking the active tab's name, by double-clicking a row in the tab list (or pressing `F2` on it), or from its menu.
- **Reorder** tabs by dragging their rows up and down the tab list, and drag a row onto a folder or out of one to move it in or out.
- **Manage** a tab from its menu: right-click a row in the tab list or the active tab, or click the **⋯** button on the active tab. The menu holds **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Lock Tab** and **Delete**, plus **Add to Folder**, **Add to Document** (share it with another document), import, export and more. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-tab).
- **Organise** related tabs into one-level folders with **Add to Folder** when a document grows. See [Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

On a view-only link the tab list still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button, no **New Tab** and no tab menu, and names cannot be changed. The active tab is never cut short, on a phone too.

For the depth, see [Tabs](/help/tabs/): the document group and the tab list are just the control surface for them.
