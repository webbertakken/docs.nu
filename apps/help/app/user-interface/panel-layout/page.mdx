Everything around the canvas is a toolbar, a **panel** or a fixed control. The canvas itself fills the screen, and the rest sits on top of it at the edges rather than taking fixed slots in a rigid layout, so what is on the canvas always has room to breathe. The layout is much the same on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

## What sits where

- **Along the top**: [the top line](/help/user-interface/title-bar/#the-top-line). At its left, the brand group: **Back** to the Explorer, the **menu button**, which opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) (your documents and folders) under it, and the tab's [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). In the middle, the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/), for picking a selection mode and adding things to the canvas; in [Sketch mode](/help/canvas/sketch-mode/) the strip gives way to its dock, which sits along the bottom unless you move it to the top. At its right, the actions group: the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Report a Bug, Settings, appearance), Share or Copy, and your account.
- **Top-right**: the AI panel and the tool panels, when they are in use, below the top line.
- **Bottom-left**: the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/), on a computer or tablet, once a tab has a few elements, just above the tools group.
- **Along the bottom**: [the bottom line](/help/user-interface/title-bar/#the-bottom-line). At its left, the tools group: Undo and Redo, the [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (**Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll**, with the Workshop control on; Timer and Vote on a Plan tab), the mode's own buttons (**Slides** in Pages mode; **Trash**, **New Card**, **Find a Card** and **Card Types** on a Plan tab, while the tab shows cards), **Collaborate** (while the tab has a comment or an action), **Layers** (not on Plan tabs or in Pages mode) and the **Theme & canvas** button side by side (no Theme & canvas button in Sketch mode), and the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). Their panels open as popovers over their buttons, and close when you press the button again. At its right, the document group: the document's name, the active tab, the tab stack and the people here, which open [the tab list](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), and **+** for a new tab.
- **Next to a selection**: the [toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) for a shape or arrow.

The tool panels (Laser, Spotlight, Eraser, Format, Avatar and Slide Deck) appear while their [selection mode](/help/selection-modes/) is in use.

## Put panels in the corner you want

With a mouse or trackpad, the corner panels can be dragged by their header to any corner of the screen: the AI Assistant and Map panels, and the tool panels. On a touch screen, tapping a panel's header collapses or expands it instead. Snap guides appear in the four corners while you drag, and the nearest one highlights to show where the panel will land; drop it away from every corner and it stays right where you left it. Panels sharing a corner stack neatly and reflow when one of them is hidden.

Your arrangement is remembered on this device, so a reload restores it. Once a panel has moved from its default spot, a **Reset position** button in its header sends it back. Each panel can also be collapsed from its header to a slim banner.

The two lines stay where they are.

## On a phone

On a phone the two lines wrap to fit, the document's name is not shown, and the strip's tiles swipe sideways. Held sideways, the phone has no menu button, and **Show Controls** at the top right brings back the actions group and the document group. Tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#on-a-phone).

[Panel Opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/) fades every panel so the canvas shows through, and **UI Scale** under **Appearance** makes them bigger or smaller (on a computer).

<Tip>
  Want even less on screen? [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) (`Z`) hides every panel and leaves
  just the canvas, the zoom controls and, with the Workshop control on or on a Plan tab, the session
  tools.
</Tip>
