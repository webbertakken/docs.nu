The **quick controls** open **the actions group**, at the right of [the top line](/help/user-interface/title-bar/#the-top-line), before the document's own actions (Share or Copy) and your account. They gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas, and they stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection. A matching cluster appears at the bottom of the full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).

## What lives here

Four controls, left to right:

- **Search**: open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any document, tab, or element, to drop something onto the canvas, or to run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it from the keyboard.
- **Report a Bug**: tell us what went wrong, without leaving the document. See [Report a Bug](/help/troubleshooting/report-a-bug/).
- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). This is the app's own chrome, not the diagram's theme, which lives behind the **Theme & canvas** button in the tools group at the bottom left. In [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) a click flips straight between light and dark instead.

The Explorer's cluster also carries a link to LiveDoc's source on GitHub, on a computer.

In the editor the controls are icons alone, and hovering one shows its name and what it does. Turn on **Button Labels** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) under **Appearance** to show each name beside its icon on a computer (the appearance switch names the mode in force: Light, Dark, or System). On a phone they are always icons alone, and so they are everywhere with Minimal Chrome on in power user mode.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives: search finds both things and commands.
