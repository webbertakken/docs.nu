The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group, just above the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/#the-timeline). Where the Timeline tells you what _happened_, the Inbox tells you what is still _open_: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) and [Plan cards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

Everything on the page comes from one of your documents, so the row always knows where it is from. The filter bar over the list picks which rows show, each filter with its count:

- **My Actions:** every open action whose assignee is you, on any document you can open. An action you assigned to yourself (the **Myself** row in the assign dialog) lives here too, so the list doubles as your to-do list across documents. Plan cards whose **Assignee** is you are here too, until the card is finished: moved to a board's done column or past it, archived, or put in the Trash.
- **Assigned by Me:** open actions you assigned to somebody else, so you can see what you are waiting _on_, not just what is waiting on you.
- **Comments:** unresolved threads you are in. That means threads you wrote a comment in, threads where a teammate [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate), and threads on documents you own. Comment threads on Plan cards are listed too, by the same rules.
- **Form Responses:** forms with responses you have not seen yet, on documents you own or reach through a team.

**All** shows every row. The Inbox remembers your choice on this device. When nothing at all is waiting, the page says **Nothing waiting on you**.

Done actions, finished cards and resolved threads are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history; the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/#the-timeline) and each document's own **Collaborate** panel keep the past.

## Reading a row

Rows are newest first, under **Today**, **Earlier This Week** and **Older**. Each row is three lines:

1. **Who is behind it** and how long ago: who assigned it to you, who it waits on, or who wrote last in the thread. The name is bold when the row asks something of you.
2. **What it is:** the action's name, the card's number and title, the element a thread is on, or the form's heading.
3. **Why it is here:** **To Do**, **Waiting on** a teammate, **Mentioned You**, **On Your Document**, **You Commented** or a count of **New Responses**, then a chip with the document's name (a team document's chip carries the team glyph).

Each kind of row is capped at 100, far more than anyone scrolls through.

## The reading pane

Choose a row and the reading pane beside the list shows where it is (the document, the tab and the element), its title, who and why in a sentence, and what you can do about it. A comment thread shows its whole conversation.

- **Reply** and **Resolve** answer a comment thread without leaving the Inbox. They are there when the document lets you comment: your own documents, your team's, and documents shared with you through a link that lets you take part. Through a view-only link the thread is yours to read, not to answer.
- **Mark Done** appears in the editor's Explorer panel for an action on the tab you have open.
- **Open** is always there: it takes you to the element in the editor.

A thread you resolve, or an action you mark done, leaves the list straight away. Plan cards, card threads and forms are answered where they live: **Open** takes you there.

## Keyboard

With the list focused, **j** and **k** (or the arrow keys) move through the rows, **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last, and **Enter** opens the chosen row. Anywhere in the Inbox, **r** starts a reply and **e** resolves or marks done. In the reply field, **⌘Enter** (Ctrl+Enter on Windows and Linux) sends it. **Esc** goes back from the reading pane to the list on a narrow screen.

## On a phone

On a phone, and in the editor's Explorer panel, the list stands alone. Tap a row and the reading pane takes its place, with **Back to Inbox** at the top; your phone's Back gesture returns to the list too. The filter bar scrolls sideways.

## Opening a row

**Open** takes you to the document on the right tab with the element selected, scrolled into view, and its action or comment popover already open, the same place a **Collaborate** panel row takes you inside the editor. From there you complete the action, reassign it, resolve the thread, or reply, with the editor's usual rules about who can edit what.

A Plan card's row opens the document on the tab with the card's board, selects the board, and opens the card, the same as clicking it on the board. A card that is not on any board opens on the document's usual tab. A form's row opens the board its responses land on.

If the element has been deleted since the page loaded, the document still opens on that tab; there is simply nothing left to select.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions and Plan cards **assigned to you**. It counts only work waiting on you, not work you handed out and not comment threads, and it shows **0** when nothing is waiting, so you can tell from any other Explorer section whether something needs you, or that you are all done.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of the canvas. Signed out, you can still assign actions to yourself and comment on your own documents, and both show up here.

**Your Inbox follows you when you sign up.** Actions you gave yourself and threads you wrote in as a guest still read as yours after you create an account, alongside the documents that move with you.

## Documents saved only in this browser

Documents saved only in This Browser (Offline Mode) show up here too: their open actions, Plan cards and comment threads are listed with everything else, and counted in the badge. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the document is kept in this browser and nowhere else; open the document to reply or resolve there. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them.

<Tip>
  Clearing this browser's site data deletes a document in This Browser, and its rows go with it.
  Sync the document to keep it on LiveDoc and see its actions from anywhere.
</Tip>
