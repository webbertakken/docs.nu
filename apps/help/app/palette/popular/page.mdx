**Popular** is the category Diagram mode's palette opens on. Its twelve tiles sit right on the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/), picked from across the palette, so the shapes and tools you use most are one click away. Each tile behaves exactly like the same tile in its home category: click it and then click the canvas to drop it, or drag on the canvas to draw it at a size, or drag the tile straight onto the canvas. Tiles preview the tab's theme the same way.

</Figure>

## What's in it

Square, Circle, Diamond, Text, Arrow, Frame, Mind Node, Flowchart, Sankey, Table, **AWS** (opens the AWS marks) and Entity.

The list is fixed and the same for everyone: using a tile never reorders it, and it can't be edited. Everything else is one pick away in the **category picker** just before the tiles, or a search away with the strip's **Search**.

## Other modes

[Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) and [Pages](/help/canvas/pages-mode/) show their categories along the strip by name instead, each opening a panel of its elements, so they have no Popular. [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/)'s palette opens on **Cards** (or **Boards**, until the tab has a board), an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) opens on its own notation, and [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) shows its own pens.

## Every time you open a document

The palette strip opens on Popular each time a document loads, rather than on the last category you used, and shows its twelve tiles (fewer on a narrow window, with the rest behind **More**).

<Tip>
  Several Popular tiles have a single-key shortcut, so you can skip the palette altogether: **R**
  for a square, **O** a circle, **D** a diamond, **T** text, **A** an arrow and **F** a frame. Hold
  **⌘** (Ctrl on Windows) to see each tile's shortcut on the palette. See [Keyboard
  Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).
</Tip>
