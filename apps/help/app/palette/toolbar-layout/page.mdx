The editor lays its tools out the same way on every device, a computer, a tablet or a phone: the palette is one strip across the top of the canvas, and the Explorer opens from a menu button in the top-left corner.

## What it does

The palette is a single strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas. From left to right it holds:

- **Selection mode**: Select, Hand, Eraser, Laser and the rest.
- In Structured mode, **the category picker** and **its tiles**: the strip opens on [Popular](/help/palette/popular/), its twelve tiles right on the strip, and the picker before them switches to any other category (Shapes, Write, Draw, Diagrams, Concepts, Layout, Icons, Tech, Media). A category with more than the strip holds ends in **More**, which opens the whole category.
- In Pages mode, and with the Workshop control on, **the categories** by name instead. Rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath.
- **Search** (the magnifier, or press **S**), last on the strip, to find any element, icon or sticker by name.

A Plan tab has no strip at all: it is worked by its boards, sheets and dashboards, which carry their own tools. See [Plan Tabs](/help/canvas/plan-tabs/).

In the top-left corner, in the brand group, sit **Back** (to the Explorer), the **menu button** and the mode switch; the tab's name rides in the active tab at the bottom right. A tab's editor mode (Sketch, Structured or Pages) is chosen on its start while it is empty, and changed from the mode switch, see [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#choosing-a-mode). The menu button opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) as a popover under it, and clicking it again closes it.

**Layers**, **Collaborate** and the Session buttons' **Poll** and **Vote** open just above their buttons in the tools group, at the bottom left, and close when you click the button again. Every other panel (the AI assistant, the Map and the tool panels) docks in a corner of the canvas, and you can drag it to another (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)). Undo and Redo stay at the start of the tools group.

Only one of the strip's menus is open at a time: opening the selection mode, a category, More or Search closes whichever other was open, and a click anywhere outside closes it.

## The categories by name (Pages and Workshop)

In Pages mode, and with the Workshop control on, each category on the strip opens a panel of its elements under its name:

- **Rest the pointer on a category** and its panel opens; slide along the strip and the panel follows to each one you point at. Move away from both the strip and the panel and it closes. A click or a tap opens it too.
- **Its elements come in named groups**, such as **Basics**, **Flowchart** and **More Shapes** in Shapes. Shapes shows them as pictures; the other categories as cards with a line on what each one is for. A tile's keyboard shortcut shows beside its name.
- **Click an element to add it, or drag it onto the canvas.** The panel closes once you have used one, so the canvas is clear. The strip at the bottom of the panel says what the element under your pointer does.
- **Card** and **Badge** open their presets in a second panel beside the first, which stays open behind it.
- **Icons**, **Stickers**, **Tech** and **Session** list their groups down the left, with the open group's entries on the right and a search box across the top that looks through every group. Each reopens on the group you last had open.
- With the keyboard: the **Left** and **Right** arrows move along the strip, **Down** or **Enter** opens a category, the arrows move through its elements, and **Escape** closes it.

The names on the strip are one word each, and the editor modes offer different categories: **Write** holds the words (Text, notes and comments) and **Draw** the pens (Freehand, Highlighter, Arrow and Line), and with the Workshop control on **Ask** puts a question to the room while **Session** holds the timers, reveals, records, reactions, selection-mode buttons and the places Avatars walk to. On a window too narrow for the names, the strip shows the categories' icons alone.

## Search

**Search** finds an element without knowing which category holds it. Before you type, it shows the kinds of element already on your tab, so the ones you are using are one click away. Type a name, or a word you would use for it ("database" finds the cylinder, a database icon and the database brands), and matching tiles, icons, stickers and technology marks appear in one list, the best name matches first; where two match equally well, the tiles your current editor mode offers lead. The × at the end of the box clears it. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the best match.

Each editor mode keeps its palette to the tools that suit it, so a Structured tab has no charts and a page has no pens. Search still finds those: they wait below, under **Elements From Other Modes**, closed until you open it, and every one of them works in any mode. That includes Sketch mode's **Marker 1, 2 and 3**, in the colours and widths you gave them there: pick one and draw, and press Escape to put it down.

Plan's own elements (its cards, boards, metrics, charts of cards and sheets) are made for Plan's boards, so only a Plan tab adds them. Elsewhere, Search lists them last, under **Not Available in Structured Mode** (or whichever mode you are in), greyed out so you can see where they live. Add a Plan tab from the **+** after your tabs to add one.

Search is not on an Event Storming board, where the notation is the palette, nor for a Participant, whose strip holds only the Participate tiles, and Sketch mode shows its own dock instead of the strip. Wherever the strip's Search isn't shown, **S** keeps its old meaning and picks the Select tool.

## The order of the tiles

Each category keeps its own order: adding something never moves it, and its groups of related tiles (such as the desktop and the mobile devices, or the charts and the meters) always come in the same order. An Event Storming board's strip opens on its notation, and a Participant's on **Participate**.

## What else the strip shows

- **Plan tabs** have no strip: its boards, sheets and dashboards carry their own tools.
- **On an Event Storming board** the strip shows the notation's tiles instead of the two pickers, led by **Add from Photo** where it is available.
- **Viewers** who can't edit have no strip, since there is nothing to add; the menu button still shows.
- **Zen mode** hides the strip and the menu button with the rest of the editor's chrome.

## On a phone

On a phone the strip is narrower:

- The **brand group** stays at the left of the top line, its mode switch an icon alone. Where the strip doesn't fit beside it, the strip takes a row of its own just under the top line.
- In Structured mode the **category picker** shows its icon only, and the category's tiles swipe sideways, the whole category on one row; the selection mode and Search stay put.
- In Pages mode, and with the Workshop control on, the **categories** show their icons only, a little further apart. Swipe them sideways to reach the rest. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Layers** and **Collaborate** open above their buttons in the tools group, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

A phone held sideways counts as a phone too, with no menu button: the screen is too short for the Explorer.
