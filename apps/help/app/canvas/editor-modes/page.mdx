An **editor mode** is how you work on a tab right now. There are five: **Diagram**, **Draw**, **Workshop**, **Pages** and **Plan**. A mode decides which tools are in front of you. You choose it from the switch beside the menu button, on any tab, or from the top of a new tab's start, and everything on the tab stays exactly where it is.

Three words describe how documents and tabs differ, and they mean different things:

- **Mode** is how a tab is worked on. It belongs to the tab, so everyone on the tab is in the same mode.
- **Kind** is what a tab is. Almost every tab is a general tab that works in every mode; the [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) is the one tab with its own kind, its own notation and no modes.
- **Template** is what a document was made from. A Kanban board or a retrospective is a general tab made from a template, never a kind of tab of its own. See [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/).

## The Five Modes

- **Diagram**: shapes, arrows, the palette and snapping. The palette offers Shapes, Write, Draw, Diagrams, Concepts, Layout, Icons, Tech and Media, and shapes snap into line with alignment guides. This is where every new document starts unless its template says otherwise.
- **Draw**: pens, the eraser and shape recognition, for sketching by hand. The palette strip gives way to Draw's own tools: Select, three markers, Text, the Path tool, the Eraser (whole strokes or just part of one) and your pinned shapes, in a dock across the top. See [Draw Mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/).
- **Pages**: pages, for building a finished visual or writing an article. The tab is laid out as sheets in a row, each with a settings cog and a **+** after the last to add another, and the palette trades pens, tech icons and Collaborate for Components, Devices and Data charts. Export from Pages saves the pages. See [Pages Mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/).
- **Plan**: boards of items, for running work through columns of cards. There is no palette: boards, sheets and dashboards carry their own tools, and a new one comes from **Start Planning**. The tools group, at the bottom left, gains **Trash**, **New Card**, **Find a Card** and **Card Types** (labelled **New**, **Find** and **Types** on a wide screen), while the Layers and Quick Style panels step aside. See [Plan Mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/).
- **Workshop**: running a session with your team, such as a retro, a town hall or a workshop. The palette adds **Stickers**, **Data**, **Ask** and **Session** (timers, votes, polls, reveals, quizzes and the rest of the session kit), and the tools group gains the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons. See [Workshop Mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/).

Every mode is on for everyone, with nothing to turn on in Settings.

## Choosing a Mode

The mode switch sits right of the menu button at the top left, on every tab, and shows the mode you are in (on a phone, its icon alone). A new, empty tab also shows it at the top of its start's list (**Start Diagramming**, **Start Drawing**, **Start a Workshop**, **Start Making Pages** or **Start Planning**); the two always agree, so a change in either shows in both. Press it to open the mode menu: the five modes down the left, the current one ticked, and beside them a preview of the mode under your pointer, with a picture, a line on what it is for and its main tools. Choosing a mode in the list (a click, or **Enter** on the highlighted one) switches the tab and its start at once; **Escape** or a click elsewhere closes the menu without switching. On a narrow screen the preview is left out and each mode shows its line under its name.

**Shift+D** moves the tab to the next mode: on an empty tab Diagram, then Draw, Workshop, Pages, Plan and back to Diagram; on a tab with something on it, the same round without Plan, and on a Plan tab it does nothing. The preview shows **⇧D** on the mode it leads to, and screen readers announce the mode you land in ("Draw mode"). Shift+D is listed under **Settings** › **Keyboard**, and it stops working with every other shortcut when you [turn shortcuts off](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/#turning-shortcuts-off).

**A tab with something on it changes mode beside the menu button.** The palette and the tab's icon follow at once, and every element stays where it is. Plan is the exception: a tab with content never moves into or out of Plan, so choosing it (or, on a Plan tab, another mode) offers a new tab in that mode instead.

## One Mode for Everyone

- **The mode belongs to the tab.** Everyone on a tab works in the same mode at once, so what you see always matches what your collaborators see.
- **Switching switches everyone.** When you choose a new mode for a tab, everyone on it follows straight away, and a message tells them who switched ("Ada switched this tab to Draw."). Anything they were typing is kept.
- **Undo switches back.** Switching is one step in your [undo history](/help/canvas/undo/): **Undo** puts the tab back in the mode it was in, exactly as it was, for everyone, and **Redo** switches again.
- **The tab shows its mode.** The active tab, and each tab in the [tab list](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), starts with the icon of its mode, the same icon the mode switch uses.
- **A tab opens in its mode**, for everyone, Diagram unless something set it otherwise.
- **New documents open in the mode you choose** in the New Document wizard, and **new tabs open in the mode of the tab you were on**, on that mode's own start (a Pages tab's **+** makes a Pages tab on **Start Making Pages**, a Plan tab's makes a Plan tab on **Start Planning**), unless the template you pick opens somewhere else: **Blank Whiteboard** opens in Draw, **Blank Illustration** in Pages, **Blank Plan** in Plan, and **Blank Session** and the session templates (the retrospectives, Town Hall Q&A, Lean Coffee, Crazy Eights and Meeting Agenda, plus the boards with a dot vote: Affinity map, Prioritization matrix and Risk matrix) in Workshop.
- In the Explorer, the **Opens in** filter finds documents by the mode they were created to open in. See [Filters](/help/explorer/filters/).

## On a Phone

A phone chooses a mode the same way, from the switch beside the menu button (its icon alone) or at the top of an empty tab's start. On a phone every mode starts on the [Hand](/help/selection-modes/hand/) tool, so a drag moves the canvas.

## Who Can Switch

- **Editors** of a general tab get the mode switch and Shift+D. **Participants** follow the tab's mode, with no switch.
- **People who can only view** a tab follow its mode, with no switch, and Shift+D does nothing for them.
- On a [locked tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/) nobody can switch: the tab stays in its mode until it is unlocked.
- **Event Storming boards** have no modes: they keep their own tools and notation, and show no switch.

Leaving **Plan** never asks: an empty tab has nothing on it to keep.

## Templates and Modes

An empty tab opens on its mode's start (**Start Diagramming**, **Start Drawing**, **Start a Workshop**, **Start Making Pages**, **Start Planning**), where that mode's templates are, and the mode switch at the top of the start's list changes the tab's mode before you begin. Every template opens in one mode. A start's search also finds the other modes' templates, each card saying which mode it opens in, and choosing one switches the tab there. Each start's **Recommended** leads with a blank of its mode, to start from nothing. See [Finding Templates for a Mode](/help/canvas/templates/#finding-templates-for-a-mode).
