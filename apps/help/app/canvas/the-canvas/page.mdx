The canvas is where you actually build diagrams in LiveDoc. It is an open, scrollable surface you can pan and zoom freely, with the palette pinned across the top of it for adding elements and switching tools. Everything works straight away, no sign-in required, so you can [open a fresh canvas](/new) and start placing shapes in seconds.

## The Palette

The **Palette** is a strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas, holding everything you add to it. It starts with the canvas tool (**Select**, **Hand**, **Laser** and the other [selection modes](/help/selection-modes/)), then the palette's categories by name, each opening a panel of its elements as you rest on it, and ends with **Search** for finding any element by name. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for the details.

In the top-left corner, the brand group holds **Back** to the Explorer, the **menu button**, which opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), and the mode switch. A tab's mode (**Diagram**, **Draw**, **Workshop**, **Pages** or **Plan**) is chosen on its start while it is empty, and changed from the mode switch: see [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#choosing-a-mode).

## Adding Elements

Most elements are added straight from the palette strip. In Diagram mode it opens on **Popular**, its twelve tiles right on the strip, and the category picker before them switches to **Shapes**, **Write** (text, notes and comments), **Draw** (the pens and arrows), **Diagrams** (mind nodes, flowcharts, tables), **Concepts** (pyramids, funnels, cycles and the Sankey), **Layout** (lanes, frames, cards), **Icons**, **Tech** and **Media**. Workshop and Pages show their categories along the strip by name instead, each opening a panel of its elements. Each mode offers its own set: **Components**, **Devices** and **Data** (charts) belong to [Pages mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/), for example. Clicking an element arms a combined gesture: a tap drops the element at your pointer, and a drag sizes it as you go. Single-key shortcuts skip the palette altogether, such as `R` for a rectangle, `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Adding Elements](/help/canvas/the-canvas/adding-elements/) for the full walkthrough.

## Moving Around

The canvas is effectively infinite, so you are never boxed in. Use the Hand tool, a middle-click drag, two fingers on a trackpad, or hold Space and drag to pan, and `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + scroll, a pinch, or the zoom controls at the bottom left to scale the view. The [Panning and Zooming](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) article covers every way to navigate.

Each tab has its own backdrop and theme. The **Theme & canvas** button beside the zoom controls opens the **Tab Style** dialog, where you can [change the canvas background](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) or [pick a theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
