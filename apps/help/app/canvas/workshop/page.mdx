**Workshop** is a control for running a session with your team: a retrospective, a town hall, a lean coffee, a planning workshop or a quick icebreaker. Turned on, it puts the tools that run a room in front of you (timers, dot votes, polls, reveals, quizzes, stickers); turned off, it keeps them out of the way while you sketch, diagram or make pages.

Workshop is not an [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/): it works on top of whichever mode the tab is in, **Sketch**, **Structured** or **Pages**, so a team can run a vote on a sketch or a poll over a page of slides. It used to be a mode of its own, **Workshop mode**, and before that **Facilitate mode**.

## Turning It On

- **Press Workshop** in the actions group at the top right, before Make a Copy, Present and Share. It is a toggle: while it is on the button is filled, and the Session strip appears. Press it again to turn it off. On a phone it shows its flipchart icon alone.
- **One control for everyone:** it belongs to the tab, so when someone who can edit turns it on, everyone on the tab gets the session kit together, and **Undo** turns it back.
- **It stays as you switch mode.** Switching between Sketch, Structured and Pages keeps it on (or off), and turning it changes no mode, no tool and nothing on the canvas.
- **New tabs take it along:** a tab added from **+** › **Tab** has the control on when the tab you made it from has, and **Duplicate** keeps it.

## What It Brings

- **The Session strip**, at the bottom left: the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons, right after Undo and Redo, in every mode. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **The Collaborate categories** in the palette: **Ask** (votes, polls, quizzes, estimates and the Q&A board), **Tools** (timer, stopwatch, reveal zone, Done check, picker), **Record** (comment panel, action card, agenda, decision record, roll call), **React** (the reaction pads), **Selection Mode** and **Navigate** (portals, Bring Focus, chairs, desks, fences and link cards). In Structured and Pages the palette's bar shows **Ask** and **Session**, which holds the other five down its side; Session's search looks through all of them. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/).
- **Stickers**, right after Icons, in Structured (Pages already has them).
- **Sketch** shows its dock rather than a palette, so there the Session strip is the kit.
- **The mode's own tools stay.** Structured keeps its Popular, Concepts, Technology and Diagrams, Pages keeps its pages' palettes, and Sketch keeps its dock.

## With It Off

- **Nothing on your tabs changes.** Session elements already on a tab keep working whatever the control: a session button still starts its timer, a reveal zone still reveals and a quiz still takes answers.
- **Structured keeps two Collaborate tiles** in its Write category, the **Comment panel** and the **Action card**, since comments and actions belong to every mode.
- **The element search still finds them.** Search for "timer" or a sticker with the control off and it is still there, and it is placed without turning anything on. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/).
- **A timer, vote or poll already running** keeps running and shows on its own canvas element; turn the control on to watch turnout, end it or keep its results.
- Comments, presence, follow-me, Presentation mode and the facilitator baton have nothing to do with the control: they are always there.

## Who Can Turn It On

- **Editors** of a general tab get the Workshop button. It is not on a [locked tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/).
- **Participants and viewers** see no button: they see the tab as it is. Participants take part in everything that is running (they vote, answer polls and add sticky notes); viewers watch.
- **Plan tabs** have no Workshop button: their strip's Timer and Vote are their own. **Event Storming boards** always have the session kit.

## Starting a Session

- **A new document:** in the New Document wizard, choose **Workshop**. The empty tab opens in Structured with the control on, on **Start a Workshop**: the session templates grouped as **Retrospectives**, **Meetings**, **Spaces**, **Quizzes & Games**, **Ideation**, **Planning**, **Strategy** and **Decisions**, with **Recommended** first. **Blank Session** (or the **×**) leaves one empty tab with the control on.
- **Session templates open in Structured with the control on:** the retrospectives (Retrospective, Start, Stop, Continue, Mad, Sad, Glad, 4Ls and Sailboat), Town Hall Q&A, Lean Coffee, Crazy Eights, Meeting Agenda, the Workshop Board and the Brainstorm Board, the boards that come with a dot vote (Affinity map, Prioritization matrix and Risk matrix), so the vote is there to run from the Session strip, and the sticky-note boards a group fills in together, such as SWOT, Empathy Map and User Journey. So do the quizzes and games: a Pub Quiz of timed [quiz](/help/palette/collaborate/quizzes/) rounds with a scoreboard, Icebreaker Questions spun on [pickers](/help/palette/behaviour/pickers/), Two Truths and a Lie and Would You Rather on live polls, and Find Someone Who Bingo. So do the spaces to walk round together in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): the Virtual Office, a Coworking Space, a Team Pod and an All-Hands Hall. Team Retro is a [Plan tab](/help/canvas/plan-tabs/), since it is a board of cards.
- **An empty Structured tab** with the control on shows **Start a Workshop** in place of Start Diagramming; Sketch and Pages keep their own starts.
- **Any tab:** turn Workshop on when the session starts, and off when you are done. Everything on it stays where it is.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone in the session, so they can vote, answer polls and add
  sticky notes, and hand the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton to whoever is
  running it, so only they start and end the tools.
</Tip>

## Take the Tour

With the control on, press Start Workshop Tour at the top right of the editor, just after the appearance switch, for a short [Workshop tour](/help/canvas/workshop/workshop-tour/): the Collaborate categories, a timer, a dot vote and a poll, the Session strip and Share. It never starts on its own, and you can take it again whenever you like.
