The Workshop tour shows you where everything for running a session lives. Like the [Structured Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It places a timer, a vote and a poll to show them and takes them away when it ends, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. When the [Workshop control](/help/canvas/workshop/) is on for a tab you can edit, press Start Workshop Tour at the top right of the editor, just after the appearance switch. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button doesn't appear on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone who can only view or take part. If you have a complex element selected, the button offers that element's tour instead; select nothing to get the Workshop tour back. It stays there after you've taken the tour, so you can press it again whenever you like.

## What It Covers

1. **Collaborate**: **Ask** and **Session** on the palette's bar, the session kit: Ask puts a question to the room, and Session holds the timers, reveals, records, reactions, selection modes and places to go. In Sketch, which has no palette, this step is left out. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/). The rest of the palette is the [Structured Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/)'s.
2. **A Timer for the Room**: a Timer on the canvas, counting down for everyone, with pause, reset and more time on it.
3. **Dot Voting**: a Vote, which gives everyone dots to put on the notes they back; the counts show when it ends.
4. **A Live Poll**: a Poll, a question to the room answered live, as yes or no, choices, a rating or open.
5. **Run the Room**: the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons in the Session strip, which run for everyone without placing anything. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
6. **Bring People In**: the **Share** button, where a Participant link lets people add notes, vote and answer without changing the rest. Left out where there is no Share button.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to run a workshop**, links to the [Workshop control](/help/canvas/workshop/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If the control is turned off or you switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Replaying the Tour

Press Start Workshop Tour again, or start it from Settings:

<Step n={3}>
